How Radex works

Radex reads text from screenshots and images, then gives you editable results to verify.

  1. Capture a screen share or upload an image. Screen shares are captured from a single frame.
  2. Extract text locally with Tesseract OCR. The first run downloads its OCR engine and language data to your browser.
  3. Review raw text and line-level confidence. Correct text before exporting.
  4. Export as Excel workbook, CSV, JSON, or plain text.

OCR can misread text, especially small, blurry, or handwritten content. Always check the result against the source.
